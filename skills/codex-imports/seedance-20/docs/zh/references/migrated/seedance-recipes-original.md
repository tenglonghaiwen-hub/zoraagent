# seedance-recipes · 类型模板 (v5.0)

本技能为七个最常见的 Seedance 2.0 内容类别提供生产就绪的提示词模板。有关每种类型的完整分解，见 [ref:genre-guides]。

---

## 1. 产品 / 电商

**目标：** 干净、聚焦、高保真产品展示。

```text
// 360° Orbit Template
A [product] on a [surface]. Smooth 360-degree orbit camera. Soft studio key light, rim highlight. Macro depth-of-field. 4K pristine detail. No text.
```

**范例：** `A glass perfume bottle on a white marble surface. Smooth 360-degree orbit camera. Soft studio key light, rim highlight. Macro depth-of-field. 4K pristine detail. No text.`

---

## 2. 生活方式 / 社交媒体

**目标：** 与 TikTok 和 Instagram 等平台相关的真实感瞬间。

```text
// Morning Routine Template
A [person] [action] in a [location]. Handheld phone perspective, slight sway. They [emotion]. [Aesthetic].
```

**范例：** `A young woman makes coffee in a sunlit kitchen. Handheld phone perspective, slight sway. She smiles as she sips the coffee. Warm, cozy aesthetic.`

---

## 3. 剧情 / 叙事

**目标：** 具有一致角色和情感重量的电影叙事。

```text
// Suspense Corridor Template
@Image1 character reference. A [character] cautiously [action] down a [scene]. They [action]. The camera follows closely behind them, handheld. The only sound is [sound].
```

**范例：** `@Image1 character reference. A man cautiously walks down a dark, narrow corridor. He holds a flashlight, its beam cutting through the darkness. The camera follows closely behind him, handheld. The only sound is his footsteps and nervous breathing.`

---

## 4. MV

**目标：** 与音轨同步的视觉动态片段。

```text
// Beat Sync Template
@Image1 artist, @Audio1 track. The artist performs in a [location]. Quick cuts sync to the drum beat at [BPM] BPM. [Camera moves] on the chorus. [Style].
```

**范例：** `@Image1 artist, @Audio1 track. The artist performs in a neon-lit warehouse. Quick cuts sync to the drum beat at 120 BPM. Whip pans and snap zooms on the chorus. Energetic and vibrant.`
